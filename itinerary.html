5 days in Palawan

A sample plan for first-time visitors. Estimated budget: ₱22,000 per person.

Day 1

Arrive in Puerto Princesa

  • Underground River tour in the morning
  • Dinner at the Baywalk food stalls
Day 2

Road to El Nido

  • Van transfer, about 5 hours
  • Sunset at Las Cabañas Beach
Day 3

Island Hopping Tour A

  • Big Lagoon and Small Lagoon by kayak
  • Grilled lunch on Shimizu Island
Day 4

Nacpan and Free Afternoon

  • Tricycle ride to Nacpan Beach
  • Optional dive or massage
Day 5

Fly home

  • Souvenir stop for cashew and dried mangoes
  • Airport transfer
